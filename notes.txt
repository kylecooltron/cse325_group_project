Title: ClimateSeer
Project Overview:

A prediction platform where you create an account and making weather / climate based predictions.
Your predictions are then compared against actual weather data and scores are given based on accuracy.
Score points and attempt to climb the leader-board. Different prediction types make it interesting.
Public leader boards update by seasons. Each month has a different leader board. Over time you can compare your year over year accuracy.
Predictions involve locations, ranges, temperature, wind, percipitation. Points will be somewhat abstract with a certain scale or rules, but accurate percentages and stats will also be provided. 
The further in the future the prediction is, the more points you stand to earn. 
Also, the more your prediction differs from the average median weather for that time / place, the more you stand to earn.


What problem does your application solve or what need does it meet?

Our application gives people a way to measure how accurate their weather predictions actually are. 
Users submit forecasts for temperature and precipitation before the target date, and the application automatically compares their predictions against real weather data. 
Users earn points based on accuracy and can track their performance over time through statistics, rankings, and historical predictions.
This helps meet the need of people being able to research and predict weather with a certain level of accuracy.
This solves the problem of something like this not existing yet.
I think it's a creative idea that will keep me more engaged than making another todo / recipe app.
Another related problem, is that different weather apps have differing forecasts, so you can start to determine which is most trustworthy, or maybe combining forecasts to make your own predictions.
It allows you to get to know your area better and get a feel for how weather systems work.

Who would use your application? 

Outdoors enthusiasts, weather watchers, sports players, photographers


What makes your idea interesting or valuable? 

It allows people who spend a lot of time outside to develop the skill of weather prediction in a fun way.
